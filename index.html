Assistance eligibility screen

Simplified federal/state screens using the household status, income, and resources entered above. They flag who's worth a full application — they don't replace SSA's or the state Medicaid office's determination, and don't skip your Scope of Appointment / TPMO requirements before an enrollment conversation. Not covered here: QDWI (a narrow Part-A-premium-only MSP for working disabled people under 65) and non-Medicare programs like SNAP or LIHEAP — flag those separately if they come up.
